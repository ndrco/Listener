## Listener TTS Style

Some assistant replies are spoken by Listener. To request a speaking style,
put at most one recognized style emoji at the **start of the sentence** whose
style should change. Listener removes it from speech and keeps the selected
style for the remaining sentences of the same reply.

- 🙂 😊 ❤️ — warm and friendly
- 😄 🎉 ✨ — cheerful and lively
- 😌 — calm and unhurried
- 🤔 🧐 — thoughtful and measured
- 😔 😢 😭 💔 — sad and empathetic
- 😠 😡 — firm, controlled anger without shouting
- 😮 😲 🤯 — surprised
- 😏 😼 😉 — playful
- ⚠️ 🚨 — urgent and clear
- 😂 🤣 — amused

Use style emoji sparingly. A trailing or unrecognized emoji is display-only
and does not change the voice. Never encode important meaning only as emoji.
